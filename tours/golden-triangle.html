Tour Overview

Experience the magic of India's most iconic circuit with our Golden Triangle Tour. This classic journey takes you through three of India's most historic cities — Delhi, Agra, and Jaipur — forming a triangle on the map that's rich in culture, architecture, and heritage.

From the bustling streets of Delhi to the timeless beauty of the Taj Mahal and the royal grandeur of Jaipur's palaces, this tour is perfect for first-time visitors to India who want to experience the essence of this incredible country in just one week.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Delhi

Welcome to India! Upon arrival at Indira Gandhi International Airport, our representative will greet you and transfer you to your hotel.

  • Check-in to 4-star heritage hotel
  • Welcome drink and briefing
  • Evening at leisure to rest and recover from jet lag
  • Optional: Evening walk through Connaught Place

Meals: Dinner | Overnight: Delhi

Day 2

Old & New Delhi Sightseeing

Full day exploring both the historic and modern faces of Delhi.

  • Morning: Visit Jama Masjid, India's largest mosque
  • Rickshaw ride through the bustling lanes of Chandni Chowk
  • Visit Raj Ghat (Mahatma Gandhi's memorial)
  • Afternoon: Drive past India Gate, Parliament House, President's House
  • Visit Qutub Minar (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  • Humayun's Tomb - inspiration for the Taj Mahal
  • Lotus Temple for meditation and peace

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Delhi

Day 3

Delhi to Agra (210 km / 4 hours)

Drive to Agra, the city of the Taj Mahal, via the Yamuna Expressway.

  • Depart Delhi after breakfast
  • En route stop at a local restaurant for lunch
  • Arrive Agra and check-in to hotel
  • Afternoon: Visit Agra Fort (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  • Explore the red sandstone fortress built by Emperor Akbar
  • Evening visit to Mehtab Bagh for sunset view of Taj Mahal from across the river

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Agra

Day 4

Taj Mahal Sunrise & Drive to Jaipur (240 km / 5 hours)

Early morning visit to witness the Taj Mahal at its most beautiful, then journey to the Pink City.

  • Pre-dawn: Sunrise visit to the Taj Mahal (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  • Spend 2-3 hours exploring this monument to eternal love
  • Learn about Shah Jahan's tribute to his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal
  • Return to hotel for breakfast
  • Drive to Jaipur with en route visit to Fatehpur Sikri (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  • Explore the abandoned Mughal city built by Emperor Akbar
  • Arrive Jaipur in evening and check-in to heritage hotel

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Jaipur

Day 5

Jaipur - The Pink City

Full day exploring Jaipur's magnificent palaces and forts.

  • Morning: Visit Amber Fort - arrive by jeep or elephant ride (optional)
  • Explore the stunning hilltop fort with its mirror palace (Sheesh Mahal)
  • Photo stop at Jal Mahal (Water Palace)
  • Afternoon: Visit City Palace - still home to Jaipur's royal family
  • Jantar Mantar - UNESCO World Heritage astronomical observatory
  • Photo stop at Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)
  • Evening free for shopping in local bazaars (textiles, jewelry, handicrafts)
  • Optional: Traditional Rajasthani dinner with folk dance performance

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Jaipur

Day 6

Jaipur to Delhi (280 km / 5.5 hours)

Return journey to Delhi with optional stops along the way.

  • Morning at leisure - optional activities or last-minute shopping
  • Visit to Albert Hall Museum or Birla Mandir (temple)
  • Check-out and drive back to Delhi
  • Arrive Delhi and check-in to hotel
  • Evening free for last-minute shopping or relaxation
  • Farewell dinner at a traditional Indian restaurant

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Delhi

Day 7

Departure from Delhi

Time to say goodbye to incredible India!

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Check-out (late check-out available on request)
  • Transfer to Indira Gandhi International Airport
  • Depart with wonderful memories of your Golden Triangle journey

Meals: Breakfast
