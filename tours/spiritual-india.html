Tour Overview

Discover the spiritual heart of India on this transformative journey through the country's most sacred sites. From the ancient ghats of Varanasi to the birthplace of Buddhism in Bodh Gaya, from the yoga capital Rishikesh to the holy Ganga Aarti at Haridwar — this tour offers deep immersion into India's timeless spiritual traditions.

Experience dawn boat rides on the Ganges, meditation sessions with learned monks, yoga by the river, and witness rituals that have been performed for thousands of years. This journey is not just sightseeing — it's an opportunity for inner reflection, spiritual awakening, and connection with ancient wisdom.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Varanasi

Welcome to the Eternal City! Varanasi, one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities.

  • Arrive at Varanasi Airport or Railway Station
  • Transfer to riverside hotel or guesthouse
  • Afternoon at leisure to settle in
  • Evening: Witness the spectacular Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat
  • Hundreds of lamps offered to the river goddess
  • Welcome dinner with traditional Banarasi cuisine

Meals: Dinner | Overnight: Varanasi

Day 2

Varanasi - City of Light

Experience the spiritual essence of Varanasi.

  • Pre-dawn (5:30 AM): Sunrise boat ride on the Ganges
  • Watch pilgrims performing rituals at the ghats
  • See the city come alive with morning prayers
  • Return to hotel for breakfast
  • Late morning: Visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple (Golden Temple)
  • Walk through the ancient narrow lanes of old Varanasi
  • Visit Banaras Hindu University and Bharat Kala Bhavan museum
  • Sarnath excursion - where Buddha gave his first sermon
  • See the Dhamek Stupa and archaeological museum
  • Evening meditation session by the river

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Varanasi

Day 3

Varanasi to Bodh Gaya (250 km / 6 hours)

Journey to the place of Buddha's enlightenment.

  • Early morning optional boat ride or yoga session
  • Drive to Bodh Gaya through rural Bihar
  • Check-in to hotel near Mahabodhi Temple
  • Afternoon: Visit Mahabodhi Temple (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  • Meditate under the Bodhi Tree where Buddha attained enlightenment
  • Walk the meditation path around the temple
  • Evening prayer ceremony at the temple

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Bodh Gaya

Day 4

Bodh Gaya Exploration

Deep dive into Buddhist pilgrimage sites.

  • Morning: Sunrise meditation at Mahabodhi Temple
  • Visit the Great Buddha Statue (80 feet tall)
  • Explore international monasteries (Thai, Japanese, Tibetan, Bhutanese)
  • Each monastery showcases unique Buddhist architecture
  • Visit Sujata Village where Buddha accepted milk rice
  • Niranjana River where Buddha bathed
  • Afternoon: Meditation and teaching session with Buddhist monk
  • Learn basics of Buddhist philosophy and practice
  • Evening chanting at Mahabodhi Temple

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Bodh Gaya

Day 5

Bodh Gaya to Delhi to Rishikesh

Fly to Delhi and drive to Rishikesh, the Yoga Capital.

  • Early morning flight from Gaya to Delhi (2 hours)
  • Drive from Delhi to Rishikesh (240 km / 6 hours)
  • Scenic drive through foothills of Himalayas
  • Check-in to riverside ashram or hotel
  • Evening orientation and welcome session
  • Attend Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat or Parmarth Niketan

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Rishikesh (Ashram)

Day 6

Rishikesh - Yoga & Ashram Life

Immerse in yogic lifestyle by the Ganges.

  • 6:00 AM: Morning yoga session (Hatha or Vinyasa)
  • Pranayama (breathing exercises) by the river
  • Breakfast - traditional ashram sattvic meal
  • Morning: Visit iconic Laxman Jhula and Ram Jhula suspension bridges
  • Explore temples and ashrams along the river
  • Swarg Ashram area for spiritual bookshops and cafes
  • Afternoon: Meditation and philosophy class
  • Learn about ancient Vedantic wisdom
  • Evening: Grand Ganga Aarti at Parmarth Niketan
  • One of the most spectacular evening ceremonies

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Rishikesh

Day 7

Rishikesh Adventure & Spirituality

Balance adventure with spiritual practices.

  • Morning: Yoga and meditation session
  • 9:00 AM: Visit Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia)
  • Where The Beatles stayed in 1968 to learn meditation
  • Explore the meditation caves and graffiti art
  • Optional afternoon activities (choose one):
  • River rafting on the Ganges (Grade II-III rapids)
  • Waterfall trek to Neer Garh Waterfall
  • Extended meditation and yoga workshop at ashram
  • Ayurvedic spa treatment and consultation
  • Evening satsang (spiritual discourse) with swami

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner | Overnight: Rishikesh

Day 8

Rishikesh to Haridwar (25 km / 1 hour)

Journey to the Gateway of Gods.

  • Morning yoga and breakfast
  • Drive to Haridwar
  • Check-in to riverside hotel
  • Afternoon: Visit Har Ki Pauri - the main ghat
  • Take holy dip in the Ganges (optional)
  • Visit Mansa Devi Temple via cable car
  • Panoramic views of Haridwar and Ganges
  • Chandi Devi Temple (time permitting)
  • Evening: Attend the famous Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri
  • Thousands gather for this powerful ceremony
  • Float diya lamps on the river
  • Farewell dinner with group

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Farewell Dinner | Overnight: Haridwar

Day 9

Departure from Haridwar

Journey's end, but the spiritual path continues.

  • Optional early morning yoga or temple visit
  • Breakfast and check-out
  • Transfer to Dehradun Airport (35 km / 1 hour)
  • Or drive to Delhi (220 km / 5 hours) for international departure
  • Depart with blessings and spiritual insights

Meals: Breakfast
